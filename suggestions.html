Resume Suggestions

Simple changes that could improve your resume.

01
Improve Professional Summary

Your summary is too general. Try mentioning your key skills, interests and career goals.

Example

"Civil Engineering student with experience in structural design, AutoCAD and project management, seeking opportunities to contribute to infrastructure projects."

02
Add Project Details

Explain your contribution, tools used and the outcome for each project.

Example

"Designed and modeled an RCC beam using Design Plus, achieving 12% material optimization compared to conventional design."

03
Include Measurable Achievements

Use numbers to show the impact of your work.

Example

"Managed a team of 4 members for a campus event, resulting in 200+ registrations."

04
Add Relevant Keywords

Consider including these keywords from the job description.

Revit BIM Project Management Excel Site Planning Structural Design
